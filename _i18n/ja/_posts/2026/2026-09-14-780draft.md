`<ViewTransition>`とFragment RefsがStable化、`browser()`とTrusted Typesのサポートを追加。

## pnpm 12.4 | pnpm
[pnpm.io/blog/releases/12.4](https://pnpm.io/blog/releases/12.4 "pnpm 12.4 | pnpm")
<p class="jser-tags jser-tag-icon"><span class="jser-tag">pnpm</span> <span class="jser-tag">ReleaseNote</span></p>
