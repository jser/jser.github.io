## htmx 4.0.0 has been released! ~ htmx
[four.htmx.org/announcements/2026-08-28-htmx-4.0.0-is-released](https://four.htmx.org/announcements/2026-08-28-htmx-4.0.0-is-released "htmx 4.0.0 has been released! ~ htmx")
